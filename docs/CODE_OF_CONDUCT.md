# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors, maintainers, and community members pledge to make participation in AI Job Agent Version 2 a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of:

- Age
- Body size
- Disability
- Ethnicity
- Gender identity and expression
- Experience level
- Nationality
- Personal appearance
- Race
- Religion
- Sexual identity and orientation
- Technical background

We are committed to fostering an open, inclusive, collaborative, and professional community.

---

# Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and professional.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Helping newcomers learn.
- Assuming good intentions.
- Focusing on technical discussion rather than personal criticism.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes.
- Demonstrating empathy toward other community members.

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Trolling or inflammatory comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Threatening language.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Deliberate intimidation.
- Repeated disruptive behavior.
- Spam or malicious content.
- Knowingly submitting malicious code.

These behaviors are unacceptable in public and private project spaces.

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories
- Issue trackers
- Pull requests
- Discussions
- Documentation
- Chat platforms
- Community meetings
- Conferences or events representing the project

It also applies whenever an individual officially represents the project.

---

# Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. Document the incident if possible.
2. Report it privately to the project maintainers.
3. Include relevant context such as:
   - Date and time
   - Description
   - Links or screenshots (if appropriate)
   - Individuals involved

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

---

# Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Clarifying acceptable behavior.
- Responding promptly to reports.
- Investigating incidents fairly.
- Taking appropriate corrective action.
- Protecting reporters from retaliation where possible.

Maintainers are expected to act impartially and consistently.

---

# Enforcement Guidelines

Depending on the severity of the violation, maintainers may take actions including:

### 1. Correction

A private explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate, along with guidance for future interactions.

### 2. Warning

A formal warning describing the violation and the expected future behavior.

### 3. Temporary Restriction

Temporary suspension from participating in project discussions or contributions.

### 4. Permanent Ban

Permanent removal from project participation for severe or repeated violations.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the severity and frequency of the behavior.

---

# Community Expectations

Contributors are encouraged to:

- Be patient.
- Be respectful.
- Keep discussions constructive.
- Focus on solving problems.
- Welcome diverse perspectives.
- Share knowledge openly.
- Help improve documentation.
- Write maintainable code.

A healthy community benefits everyone.

---

# Conflict Resolution

When disagreements arise:

1. Discuss the issue respectfully.
2. Focus on facts and technical reasoning.
3. Avoid personal criticism.
4. Seek consensus where possible.
5. Ask maintainers to mediate if needed.

Professional communication should always take priority.
